# Teaching-use permissions in practice

[License](../LICENSE.md) · [Attribution and separate licenses](../NOTICE.md)

The kit is source-available for teaching use; it is not offered under an unrestricted
open-source license. These examples explain the custom license; the license controls.

| Activity | Permission for covered Kit Materials |
| --- | --- |
| Adapt the two guides to your curriculum and share them free with your class | Allowed; retain attribution/license and identify changes |
| Publish your teaching adaptation free on your website or GitHub | Allowed under the same teaching-use conditions |
| Teach as a paid teacher, or use the guides in a normally tuition-funded course | Allowed, including sharing materials with enrolled learners through an LMS; do not disguise a guide sale as tuition |
| Have students use their own paid AI subscription | Allowed; the provider's service is separate |
| Sell a rebranded plugin, prompt pack, paid guide subscription or bundled kit | Not permitted by this license |
| Charge specifically for access to an adapted guide | Not permitted by this license |
| Use a learner-created email, analysis, plan or code in a job/business | Not restricted by this kit license; other rights/provider terms can still apply |
| Adapt and share the current default curriculum for teaching | Allowed under its teaching-use notice; prior CC BY rights in unchanged material remain valid regardless of the copy used |
| Rely on a prior CC BY grant for the same curriculum material | Those rights remain, including commercial reuse and lawful redistribution with attribution |
| Use Big Lever's name/logo as your school's branding | Not granted; use your own identity and retain attribution |

The no-resale restriction attaches to the kit's guide instructions, recipe/code,
original documentation and adaptations. It cannot revoke permissions already granted
for the curriculum material, regardless of which copy is used. Keep curriculum and
kit notices separate in your release. This distribution uses teaching-use terms;
material with existing broader rights retains those alternative permissions.
The curriculum-only repository remains a different educational package.

A future publisher must have rights to its own inputs and publish its own deployment
policies. This license grants no access to private student records or platform accounts.
For uses outside these permissions contact help@bigleverinstitute.org.
